## 2025-02-27 - [UI Aesthetic Insight]
**Learning:** Found raw hex color codes and arbitrary inline `background` styles (like `rgba(9,11,18,0.9)`) being used for elements in `TradePlan.tsx` which violated the design system boundary, resulting in missing glassmorphism effects and potential inconsistencies with core palette tokens. Also noticed numerical values not having monospace `tabular-nums` formatting.
**Action:** Refactored `TradePlan.tsx` to use semantic tailwind tokens (mapped via tone props like `"emerald"`, `"blue"`, `"rose"` for `text`, `border`, `bg`). Replaced solid backgrounds with `#0a0a0c`/80 with `backdrop-blur-md` to enforce the MP-17 dark-glassmorphism standard and added `font-mono tabular-nums` to relevant `<p>` and `<span>` elements for typography consistency.
